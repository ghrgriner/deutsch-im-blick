# Overview

This project has three objectives. All objectives use vocabulary
presented in the _Deutsch im Blick_ German textbook and its
companion grammar (details later in this overview). The
objectives are:

1. Provide a vocabulary deck with audio for learners who wish to use
   _Deutsch im Blick_ as their text book.

2. Provide an open-source vocabulary list used in a first-year course for
   a language, where the words are linked to Wiktionary pages and
   senses. This might be useful to learners (or teachers) who wish
   to create a first-year course in a different language.

3. Provide statistics by language of translation completeness. That is,
   we assess what proportion of the words have a Wiktionary
   translation available and provide programs for users to extract
   these translations (but not fully transclude the wikitext in the
   translations).

## Source of vocabulary list

*Deutsch im Blick* (2nd edition, 2017) and *Grimm Grammar* are a free
online first-year German textbook and companion grammar authored by
Dr Zsuzsanna Abrams and published by the Center for Open Educational
Resources and Language Learning (COERLL) at the University of Texas-Austin.
The vocabulary list in this repository contains of vocabulary and
matching audio files (when available) from the end of each chapter in
*Deutsch im Blick*. Selected elements of *Grimm Grammar* or vocabulary
introduced in the chapter text are also included.

* URL of the *Deutsch im Blick* homepage: https://coerll.utexas.edu/dib/

* URL of the *Grimm Grammar* homepage: https://coerll.utexas.edu/gg/

Selected information from the English-language Wiktionary is also
included.
