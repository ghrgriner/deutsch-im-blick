'Deutsch in Blick' and 'Grimm Grammar' attribution
--------------------------------------------------
Deutsch im Blick (2nd edition, 2017) and Grimm Grammar are a free
online first-year German textbook and companion grammar authored by
Dr Zsuzsanna Abrams and published by the Center for Open Educational
Resources and Language Learning (COERLL) at the University of Texas-Austin.
This repository primarily consists of vocabulary and matching audio files (when available)
from the end of each chapter in Deutsch im Blick. Selected elements of Grimm Grammar
or vocabulary introduced in the chapter text are also included.

URL of the Deutsch im Blick homepage: https://coerll.utexas.edu/dib/

URL of the Grimm Grammar homepage: https://coerll.utexas.edu/gg/

Deutsch im Blick and Grimm Grammar are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

This derivative work is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. This is a modification of the
original work. This repository is not affiliated with or endorsed by Dr. Abrams or COERLL.

The files contain a field with the relevant chapter(s) from
which the headword(s) for each note was taken. Example phrases or
sentences may be provided. If the sentence is taken from a different
section of the text, then the relevant chapter is listed (e.g., '[Ch4]').
There are a small number of example phrases created by author of this
repository and not from Deutsch in Blick / Grimm Grammar. These are
marked with '[*]'. Similarly, the `en1_xsense` field is created by the author of
this repository. This field is to indicate the sense of a headword when there
is some ambiguity.

- We present plural forms from nouns when available in the textbook or grammar
 pages, but we often present just the suffixes, while the textbook often presents
 the full word.

- We usually attempt to present one note per sense / meaning. This means we sometimes
 combine words presented in different chapters in the vocabulary list or grammar pages
 on a single note. This also means a word that might be presented on a single line
 in the textbook or grammar generates multiple notes. For example 'das Schloss' is
 on a separate note for 'lock' and 'castle', even though both translations appear on
 a single line in the Chapter 6 vocabulary list.

 We sometimes indicate in the `de3` field the other senses that were presented in
 the textbook or grammar. Sometimes we do not create notes for all senses and we
 indicate that in `de3`.

 The source location(s) for the words/translations are given in the `chapter_list` variable.

 Although the above paragraph is the goal, we sometimes leave two German words on
 separate notes, even if their meaning or some of their meanings are the same.
 One reason for this is to keep individual cards short.

- The audio files in input/audios are extracted from the MP3 files in 'Deutsch im Blick'
 and 'Grimm Grammar'. The file names start with 'DiB-Dxx', where xx is the
 textbook chapter. 'Grimm Grammar' pages are also assigned a Chapter 1-10, as
 specified in the repository README.md or wiki.

License and Attribution information for English Language Wiktionary.
--------------------------------------------------------------------

The file contains selected information from the August 2026 dump file
of the English Language Wiktionary from:
    https://dumps.wikimedia.org/enwiktionary/20260801/

In particular, it contains for selected pages information related to
the relevant translation table(s) for a given headword. This information
includes the line generating the header of the translation table,
the level-3 and level-4 headers (if applicable) under which the translation
table appears (for the purposes of extracting Part of Speech), and a
True/False indicator whether a translation exists for the selected senses
for selected languages.
